01 · What we check

Five pricing practices that UK consumer law bans

Kestrel looks for these practices on each capture. Each finding cites the rule: the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (DMCC Act), Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) guidance, or the Price Marking Order.

  1. 01

    Drip pricing

    The headline price is lower than the final price because of mandatory fees, such as a booking fee or a service fee.

    Example£24.00 → £27.50+ booking fee £3.50
  2. 02

    False urgency

    Countdowns and “ends tonight” claims, where the price does not change when the offer ends.

    ExampleOffer ends in 00:00:09Next day: £49.00, no change
  3. 03

    Misleading reference prices

    “Was” prices and recommended retail prices (RRPs) that give a false idea of the saving.

    ExampleWas £60.00Now £30.00
  4. 04

    Pre-selected extras

    Paid add-ons that the journey adds unless the customer removes them.

    Example✓Ticket protection£2.95, ticked by default
  5. 05

    Unit pricing

    The price per unit, such as per kilogram or per litre, under the Price Marking (Amendment) Order rules.

    ExampleCoffee, 500 g · £3.20Unit price: £6.40 per kg

02 · How it works

From the offer page to the stop point

A journey is a fixed path through your storefront. Kestrel follows it as a customer would, and it never goes past the stop point.

  1. 1Offer pageHeadline price
  2. 2OptionsExtras and claims
  3. 3BasketFinal price
  4. ■Stop pointKestrel stops here
  5. ×Details and paymentNever entered

  1. 01Run

    Kestrel runs each journey on a schedule. It always stops before the first step that asks for personal details or payment.

  2. 02Capture

    Each capture keeps screenshots, a network capture and a page snapshot for each step, with the date and time.

  3. 03Review

    A person reviews each finding. Each finding cites the rule from the DMCC Act, CMA guidance or the Price Marking Order.

04 · Why now

A one-off audit cannot show that a countdown was false.

Only repeated captures of the same offer over several days show whether the price really changed when the offer ended. That is why Kestrel monitors.
