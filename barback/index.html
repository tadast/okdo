How it works

From venue to account card.

We read public company records and published menus. Then we join them up, so you see who runs each venue and how many sites sit behind one decision.

  1. 01

    Venue

    One bar, restaurant, pub or hotel. We find it and read its published menu.

  2. 02

    Operator

    The company that runs the venue. We link the two from Companies House records.

  3. 03

    Group

    The company at the top that holds the operators. Its site count is the number of UK venues it runs.

  4. 04

    Account card

    One page for each group that fits you, with the evidence and the route to the decision maker.

One win at a group gives orders from many sites.

So Barback shows you groups, not single venues.

How we work

Public sources. Lawful routes.
