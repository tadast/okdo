DoReMingo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

DoReMingo is an ear-training game published by OK Do! Ltd. We have built it to collect no personal data at all. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Data we collect

None. DoReMingo does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no contact forms.

Network and tracking

The app does not send any data off your device. It makes no network requests to us or to anyone else, and contains:

  • no analytics or usage tracking;
  • no advertising;
  • no cookies or third-party SDKs;
  • no crash or telemetry reporting.

All sounds and content are bundled inside the app and play entirely offline.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (such as which levels you have completed) locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed if you delete the app.

Children's privacy

DoReMingo is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this page and revise the date above before the change ships.
